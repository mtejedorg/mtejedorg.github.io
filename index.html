What I do

Engines, native code, and the agents that now help me write both

Most of my work sits where a product idea meets hardware limits: a headset that overheats, a store that rejects a build, a UI that should change without a release.

Unity engine and gameplay

C# and Unity since 2017: hierarchical state machines, component-based combat, ScriptableObject architecture, Zenject, Addressables, UniTask, Photon Fusion 2 multiplayer, and UI that is generated from data rather than built by hand.

XR on mobile-class hardware

Meta Quest, PICO and Android XR with Meta XR SDK and OpenXR: frame budgets at 72/90/120 Hz, Compositor Layers, thermal and battery limits, passthrough and anchors, and prototypes on Meta Spatial SDK, VIVE Wave and XREAL. On the Oculus Go that bought about 25% longer before thermal shutdown; on Samsung phones, about 20% more battery life.

Native multi-platform SDKs

A C++ core with Java and Kotlin bridges under a Unity layer, Vulkan and OpenGL ES rendering plugins that push video buffers straight to the GPU, and ExoPlayer customized at the JNI level. Shipped on Android, iOS, Android TV and headsets.

AI agents, in products and in the workflow

A multi-agent assistant on Google ADK with RAG over video embeddings, in production within a month. Day to day: Claude Code skills, Unity MCP and coding agents, and internal workshops on how to work with them.

Leading while still shipping

Started alone, hired the eight engineers of the Xtadium team and ran it: 1:1s, reviews, coding standards, roadmaps for executives and clients. Kept writing the SDK, the UI and the performance work throughout.
